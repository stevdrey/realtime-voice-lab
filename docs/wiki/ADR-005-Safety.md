# ADR-005 — Privacy and bounded spend
Status: Accepted (planning), 2026-11-23.

Context: personal credits and recordings must be protected. Alternatives: unrestricted lab, platform billing alerts only, deterministic local caps. Decision: one active session, explicit connect, short deadlines, bounded resources, env-only key, redacted memory-only logs and offline tests. Implement baseline #17 before paid smoke. Consequence: deliberately short experiments; estimates are not exact bills and provider retention still applies. Review caps after measured usage.
